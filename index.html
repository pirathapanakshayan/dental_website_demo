Your first visit, step by step

If you haven't seen a dentist in years, that's fine. Here's exactly what happens.

  1. 1

    A chat, not a chair

    We start in a normal room. Tell us what's bothering you and anything that makes you nervous.

    10 minutes
  2. 2

    Full check-up

    Teeth, gums, mouth cancer screening and bite. We show you everything on a screen as we go.

    20 minutes
  3. 3

    X-rays if you need them

    Only if there's a reason. Low-dose digital, results in seconds.

    5 minutes
  4. 4

    A written plan with prices

    You leave with your options, what each one costs and how urgent it is. No pressure to decide that day.

    You keep the copy
